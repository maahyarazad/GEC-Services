> Companion to `Savr-Development-Timeline.md` — the operational and marketing track that runs alongside the technical build, culminating in the event/app **launch on 8 Sept 2026**.
> **Owner:** **Iris** (every task in this phase). Rafael, Maahyar and Jan appear only as coordination touchpoints — they are not owners of these tasks.
> Same table structure as `Savr-Development-Timeline.md`: **Owner**, **Est. (d)**, **Start**, **End** and **Depends on** are already filled in from the plan below; **Status** and **Notes** are left blank for you to update as the phase progresses.

---

## How to use this document

- **Owner** is fixed to **Iris** for every task — unlike the development timeline, there is no owner choice to make here.
- **Est. (d)** = estimate in working days. Every listed action is treated as a **single working day** (Start = End); extend it if a real task ends up running longer than its listed day.
- **Start** / **End** are taken directly from the operational plan, in `DD-MM-YYYY` format — unchanged from the dates given.
- **Depends on** records the **immediately preceding task** in the day-by-day plan (straightforward chronological sequence) — edit it if the real dependency differs.
- **Status** suggestion: `todo` / `wip` / `blocked` / `review` / `done`.
- Fill in **Status** and **Notes** as the plan progresses.
